Privacy Policy

Effective date 2026-11-23

Scope

This policy covers Long Screenshot & PDF Scan and its support website. The app has no developer account requirement and contains no advertising, tracking or developer analytics service.

On-device content and permissions

Photos, screenshots, recordings, PDFs, recognized text, translations and edits you create or select are processed on the device and stored in the app’s project library. The camera is used when you choose to capture a scan. You choose photos and files through the system pickers. Exported, shared and backed-up files go to the destinations you select.

Translation and model downloads

The app’s offline translation engine runs on your device. With your confirmation, the app requests language models from Mozilla’s distribution service. Requests may disclose standard connection details such as your IP address. Downloads do not include your documents, source text or translations. In-app translation using Apple TranslationSession also runs on the device. Apple may collect API usage and performance metrics such as the app identifier and language pair.

Optional iCloud

In versions that offer project sync, turning it on sends originals, text, translations and project edits to your private iCloud account through Apple CloudKit. The developer does not receive the contents of your projects. You can turn sync off in Settings and manage iCloud data through Apple’s iCloud settings.

Retention and deletion

Projects stay on your device until you delete them. You can recover projects from Recently Deleted for 30 days or permanently delete them there. You can clear preview caches and remove offline language models. Manage shared copies, external backups and iCloud data where you stored them.

Website and support email

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security. The site uses no developer analytics, ads or forms. If you email the developer, your address and the information you choose to send are used to respond and troubleshoot, and are kept as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Contact support to ask to access, correct or delete the information you sent us.
