Make one clear document from many pieces.

Stitch scrolling captures, scan multipage PDFs, and keep the original and translated text together. For iPhone and iPad.

Support

  • Stitch long screenshots

    Choose images, files or frames from a screen recording. Use suggested overlaps, adjust seams directly, and export a long image or paginated PDF.

  • Scan multipage PDFs

    Capture or import several pages. Review automatic edge detection, adjust corners and curved pages, and batch-apply color, grayscale or black-and-white enhancement.

  • Translate and keep both languages

    Recognize a whole page or selected text. Choose Apple Translation or the app’s offline engine. Preview and export translated text directly beneath the original. Availability depends on language models.

  • Split and merge PDFs

    Split by page range or reorder files and merge them. Choose Original, Standard or Small File when exporting.

  • Organize and back up

    Sort projects into folders, rename them and keep editing. Export an editable backup that retains the source files.
