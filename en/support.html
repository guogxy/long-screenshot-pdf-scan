Subscriptions and restoring purchases

This app requires a Scan Pro subscription. Choose a monthly or annual plan. Eligible subscribers can try every feature free for seven days. Your subscription renews at the displayed price after the trial. To avoid renewal, cancel in Apple Account settings at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Already subscribed? Choose Restore Purchases on the opening screen. Your saved projects stay where they are after your subscription ends. Resubscribe to resume editing.

A model won’t download

In Settings → Language Models, check your translation engine, connection and available storage. Installed models work offline.

Change a scan crop

Open the project, choose the crop tool and adjust the corners. Preview the updated page before exporting.

Preview and export

Open Export in the editor to choose the file type and size. Use an editable backup to preserve the originals and edit history.
